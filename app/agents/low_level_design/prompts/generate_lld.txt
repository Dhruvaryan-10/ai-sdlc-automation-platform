INSTRUCTIONS
1. The HLD is the primary technical source. Every component, service, interface, data store, and interaction in this LLD must trace back to something in the HLD. The BRD is supporting business context. The draft user stories, when present, are additional functional context for the requirement-to-implementation mapping only.
2. Do NOT invent requirements, components, integrations, or constraints that are not present or reasonably implied by the HLD/BRD. Where the HLD leaves an implementation choice open, make a reasonable, clearly-labeled decision. Prefix any such choice with "**Design Decision:**" or "**Assumption:**". Never present an assumption as a confirmed requirement.
3.
